# Task 049 — Pay: the period home and ⚙ Pay settings

**Status: built 2026-10-28, not committed; waiting on the owner's review (built ahead of review, at the owner's ask).**

## The goal

Pay opens on this period's people and what each is owed, grouped by what needs you; the setup moves behind ⚙. The payslip, its decisions and settling (Steps 21-23) stay as they are.
RFC: [Backoffice v2 refinement](https://app.notion.com/p/40595a7154058188873dd594ee4b7875), Core 2; Step 49.

## The plan

1. **`GET /tenants/{tenant}/pay/periods/{start}`** (`current` works; any day finds its period): the dates, the pay date, the period before's first day and the next one's (none after today's), what is owed (approved, not yet paid or skipped), how many people have something to review, and every payout overlapping the period. Reuses `ListPayouts`; a period after today's is `period_not_found` (both languages)
2. **The payslip is found by period and person** (`/pay/<start>/<userId>`), through the period's own query, instead of a new `/people/{userId}` endpoint: the period lists each payout with its person, and is usually in the cache from the home. Decisions and settling still go by payout id, unchanged
3. **Pay home** (`pay/Period.tsx`): a slim header (‹ dates ›, pays on / days left, owed · to review), then To review, Ready, Paid and skipped. Non-sellers with nothing owed or waiting are left out, as v1's list did. From the current period, a card leads to the one before while anyone in it is unpaid: pay day lands there
4. **⚙ Pay settings** (`/pay/settings`): People (staff pay info, staff commissions), Types (commission, claim), Schedule. The setting screens move under it unchanged
5. **Gone:** the old home's tiles and the Staff Payouts list (past periods replace it; `GET /pay/payouts` stays in Go for now, unused)
6. **Guests:** the payslip's decisions show but are disabled; the day buttons, Pay / Skip, the staff save and the schedule switch are hidden or inert
